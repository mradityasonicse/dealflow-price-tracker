# DealFlow — Price Tracker & Auto-Coupon Engine

DealFlow is a Chrome extension for Amazon and Flipkart that tracks historical prices, displays 90-day price trends directly on product pages, and automatically finds and tests coupon codes at checkout.

## Key Features

- **In-Page Price History Chart**: Visualizes 90-day price trends, all-time low, high, and average prices directly beneath product pricing.
- **Price Drop Alerts**: One-click alert setup to monitor target prices.
- **Auto-Coupon Applier**: Automatically scans and tests merchant discount codes at checkout with a live progress modal.
- **Affiliate Monetization Ready**: Built-in support for Amazon Associate Tags and Flipkart Affiliate IDs configurable via the extension settings.
- **Manifest V3 Architecture**: Lightweight, responsive, and privacy-first.

## Installation

1. Download the pre-built package:  
   👉 **[Download dealflow-price-tracker.zip](https://github.com/mradityasonicse/dealflow-price-tracker/releases/latest/download/dealflow-price-tracker.zip)**
2. Unzip the downloaded file.
3. Open Chrome and navigate to `chrome://extensions`.
4. Enable **Developer mode** in the top right.
5. Click **Load unpacked** and select the unzipped directory.

## Customizing Your Affiliate Tags

1. Click on the DealFlow toolbar icon and open **Settings**.
2. Enter your personal **Amazon Associate Tag** and **Flipkart Affiliate ID**.
3. Click **Save Affiliate Configuration** to ensure shopping commissions are routed to your account.
